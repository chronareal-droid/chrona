03 / Recycling solutions

Recycling that runs itself.

A full turnkey recycling solution. We build the infrastructure on your site, employ the people who sort it, and remove everything, on a flat, fixed monthly service fee.

We start with a full analysis of your historical waste records and propose the best solution for you. Then a Bekabee employee, based on your site full-time, separates recyclables from non-recyclables into the various grades and streams, and it all leaves your premises.
