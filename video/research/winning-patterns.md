**Hook formula (first ~60–90 s), identical shape in all 6:**
1. Exact time + weekday + mundane place + small object in your hand ("It's [early time] on a [weekday] morning and you're standing in/at ...").
2. 2–3 sensory details that make you look *poor/ordinary* (old item, cheap price, how long you've had it).
3. A contrast character (coworker/friend) who *looks* richer.
4. The reveal: one precise hidden number (your real net worth vs. theirs).
5. "Nobody knows" line, then a one-sentence promise: "This is the story of how that happened, and what it cost you along the way."

**Body:** numbered "Level one / Level two ..." chapters, a new one roughly every 2.5–3.5 min (4–6 levels). Each level = an age + a money situation + one small decision + one exact number + a reaction from the contrast character. Slow, unglamorous progress is the point ("the years nothing happened").

**Ending:** callback to the opening object/scene, then CTA: "If this story made you think differently about ..., hit like and subscribe. There are more stories like this one coming. And I'm curious: [personal reflective question]? Tell me in the comments."

**Music:** half the videos show light music tags, half none. Narration carries everything.

## Thumbnails (all 5 follow one template)
- **No text at all.** The title does the talking.
- **One simple protagonist**, very plain cartoon face (minimal features, calm closed smile), centered or center-right, walking toward the camera or handing something over.
- **Split contrast in one scene:** left = loud fake wealth (sports car, designer bags, selfies, phones out); right = quiet real wealth (laptop with a rising portfolio chart, classic house, property deed, brokerage app).
- Bright, saturated, clean digital cartoon with lots of background detail; warm daylight or golden hour.
- Duration badge shows ~17–22 min.
