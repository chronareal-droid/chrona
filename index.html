Why Homeowners Choose Greenfields

Greenfields Instant Lawns installs instant lawns and creates landscaped gardens for homeowners who want their outdoor space done properly, from the ground up.

    Neat, organised work from preparation to clean-up, so the result looks right on day one.

  • Reliable service

  • Beautiful, long-lasting results

    Gardens planned and installed to keep looking good well beyond the day we leave.
