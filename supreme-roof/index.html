01 — The problem

Your roof doesn't have to look broken to have a problem.

A leak is a symptom. The roof needs an answer. These are the signs worth having a professional look at before they turn into ceilings, interiors and contents.

  • 01

    Water leaks

    The warning sign
    Drips, stains or wet patches after rain.
    What it could mean
    Water is getting past the roof covering, flashing or waterproofing somewhere, often not directly above the drip.
  • 02

    Damp ceilings

    The warning sign
    Brown rings, bubbling paint or a musty smell.
    What it could mean
    Slow, ongoing water ingress that has already reached the ceiling boards.
  • 03

    Cracked tiles

    The warning sign
    Cracked, chipped or slipped tiles.
    What it could mean
    Openings that let water reach the underlay and timbers below.
  • 04

    Failed waterproofing

    The warning sign
    Lifting seams, blisters or bare patches.
    What it could mean
    The membrane is delaminating or degrading and no longer sealing the roof.
  • 05

    Ponding water

    The warning sign
    Water standing on a flat roof long after rain.
    What it could mean
    Falls or drainage need attention; standing water accelerates wear on the membrane.
  • 06

    Damaged flashing

    The warning sign
    Loose, rusted or cracked flashing at walls, chimneys and edges.
    What it could mean
    The most leak-prone junctions on the roof are exposed.
  • 07

    Deteriorating coatings

    The warning sign
    Chalky, faded, cracking or peeling roof coatings.
    What it could mean
    UV and weather have worn through the protective layer.
  • 08

    Blocked drainage

    The warning sign
    Overflowing gutters or blocked outlets during storms.
    What it could mean
    Water backing up under tiles or over edges and into walls.
  • 09

    Roof ageing

    The warning sign
    Brittle materials, moss, sagging lines, repeated small repairs.
    What it could mean
    The roof may need maintenance, waterproofing or renovation rather than another patch.
  • 10

    Storm damage

    The warning sign
    Displaced tiles, dents or debris after a Highveld storm.
    What it could mean
    Hidden damage that lets the next storm in.

Please don't climb onto the roof yourself. Take photos from the ground or inside and send them to us. We'll tell you what we see.

13 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Can't see your question? Call 067 817 3343 or send us your roof details.

How do I know if my roof needs waterproofing?

Damp patches or stains on ceilings, water marks after rain, cracked or slipped tiles, ponding water on a flat roof, and coatings that are cracking or peeling are all signs worth having assessed. A roof can have a problem long before it looks broken.

How long does roof waterproofing last?

It depends on the roof, the system used, exposure and maintenance. We recommend the right system for your roof after an assessment. Our work carries a 5-year workmanship guarantee, alongside the manufacturers' product warranties.

Do you repair leaks?

Yes. We find and repair leaks on tiled, flat, slate and metal roofs, and we offer an emergency call-out service for urgent leaks.

Do you work on flat roofs?

Yes. Flat roof waterproofing is a core service, including torch-on membrane systems, screed work and liquid-applied coatings, chosen to suit the roof.

Do you work on tiled roofs?

Yes. We repair leaks, replace damaged tiles and waterproof tiled roofs, including flashing and valley areas.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We waterproof, repair and maintain commercial and industrial roofs, including offices and factory roofs.

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes. We offer a 24-hour emergency call-out service for urgent leaks. Call 067 817 3343.

Is there a warranty?

Our work carries a 5-year workmanship guarantee, and materials carry their manufacturers' product warranties. Terms and conditions apply.

How does a roof assessment work?

Tell us about your roof by phone, WhatsApp or the assessment form, with photos if you have them. We inspect the roof, identify the source or risk area and recommend the appropriate repair, waterproofing or maintenance approach.

What areas do you service?

Johannesburg and surrounding Gauteng areas, including Sandton, Bryanston, Randburg, Rivonia, Fourways, Northcliff and Johannesburg North.
